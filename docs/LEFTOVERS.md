(add on find, delete on fix) is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

own, with the bench in [development/bench.md](development/bench.md).

| 56 | Speed: the Values smoothing (median 5x5, then two guided filter passes) could be the Fast Global Smoother of OpenCV ximgproc (Apache-2.0, about 150 lines to port).
